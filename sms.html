SMS Program

DVOTD Inc. · Effective October 23, 2026

The Conference Companion is a text-message service provided by DVOTD Inc. to attendees of participating events. DVOTD Inc. is the sender of every message. The event is the context for the messages, not a party sending them. This page describes how attendees join, what they receive, and how they leave.

How attendees opt in

  • Keyword. The attendee texts the event's keyword (for example, JOIN) to the DVOTD Conference Companion number shown on event materials.
  • QR code. The attendee scans the QR code displayed on event signage, slides, badges, or emails. It opens a pre-filled text message, or a short mobile form that asks for their mobile number and an unchecked box agreeing to receive messages.
  • Sign-up form. The attendee enters their number on the event's registration or sign-up form and ticks an unchecked consent box.

Consent is never pre-selected and is not a condition of attendance. Every method shows the sender (DVOTD Inc., DVOTD Conference Companion), that message frequency varies, up to 8 msgs/day during the event, that message and data rates may apply, and how to reply STOP or HELP.

What the conversation looks like

JOIN
DVOTD Conference Companion: Welcome! You're set up for the Spring Summit. Msg frequency varies, up to 8 msgs/day during the event. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help.
One question before we start: what did you come to the Spring Summit to achieve? A sentence is plenty.
I want to find a better system for following up with leads after the event.
Got it. Two sessions tomorrow speak to that directly: "Follow-up That Sticks" at 10:15 in Hall B and the roundtable at 2:00. I'll remind you 20 minutes before each. Reply STOP anytime.

What attendees receive

  • A one-time confirmation message to verify the number.
  • Session recommendations matched to what they said they came for.
  • Session reminders and schedule updates for the event.
  • Short check-ins during and after the event, and a follow-up that carries their plan forward.

Frequency: varies, up to 8 msgs/day during the event; occasional before and after.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP to cancel. DVOTD Inc. sends one final confirmation, then nothing further.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email jasmine@getdvotd.com.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Full details are in the Privacy Policy. The program's terms are in the Terms & Conditions.
