Program description

The Conference Companion is a text-message service that DVOTD Inc. provides to attendees of participating events. DVOTD Inc. is the sender of every message. When you opt in, DVOTD Inc. confirms your number, asks what you came to the event to achieve, and sends you session reminders, schedule updates, and personalized recommendations for the event you're attending. Messages may be written with the help of AI and are reviewed by DVOTD Inc.

Opting in

You join by texting the event's keyword to the DVOTD Conference Companion number, by scanning the event's QR code and completing the form it opens, or by entering your mobile number on a sign-up form and ticking an unchecked box agreeing to receive messages from DVOTD Inc. Consent is never pre-selected and is not a condition of attendance. DVOTD Inc. sends a one-time confirmation message to verify your number before any other message is sent.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies, up to 8 msgs/day during the event. Before and after the event, messages are occasional.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. DVOTD Inc. will send one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent. To rejoin, opt in again using the same method.
